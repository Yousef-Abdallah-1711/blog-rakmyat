# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners exploring software solutions who want to understand how websites, mobile apps, custom business systems, automation, and integrations can improve their work.

## Product Purpose

Raqmiat designs and builds software around business workflows, connecting customer experiences with the systems that support day-to-day operations. This page is an Arabic blog archive that helps prospective clients find practical ideas relevant to their work and discover Raqmiat's services.

## Positioning

Raqmiat's stated approach brings customer experience, business processes, and supporting systems together in connected software shaped around the client's workflow.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a set of articles, narrow them by topic, and can continue to the project contact action. The archive currently contains six sample articles, not published or verified editorial content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML page with responsive layout, client-side category filters, and a mobile navigation menu.
- Arabic, right-to-left reading direction.
- Keep the archive to six dummy article cards; there are no single-article pages.
- The business offers websites and commerce, mobile apps, custom business systems, AI and automation, and integrations.
- The contact destination and article destinations are placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: رقميات (Raqmiat).
- The archive uses Arabic copy and right-to-left layout.
- The user specified the blue palette: main and primary `#0066ff`, secondary `#02b5ff`, and blue accents `#4a00e1`, `#5842bc`, `#157efb`, `#6640f4`, `#8169f1`, and `#291554`.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-provided pasted copy describing Raqmiat's services and workflow-first software approach.
- The six article headlines and summaries in `index.html` are dummy content for the archive prototype.
- No verified article content, customer proof, or supplied image assets are present.

## Product Principles

- Start from real workflows and business needs.
- Connect customer experience with the operations that support it.
- Make the archive easy to scan and filter by topic.
- Keep sample content clearly separate from verified business evidence.
